    The road to the platinum

    One playthrough, about 200 hours, no online trophies and no mission replay. Play the story first — only one thing can be missed — then work the 100% checklist, then clean up the rest. Keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter.

      Before you start

      • Cheats switch trophies off — and saving too. Keep them off for a trophy run.
      • No mission replay, no New Game+. There are 15 manual save slots: keep one at the start and end of each chapter and rotate the rest for free-roam cleanup.
      • Only one trophy is potentially missable: Positive Mental Attitude — the Chapter 1 meditation at Jason's house.
      • Five trophies have no verified method yet at launch (In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin). Their guides say so plainly instead of guessing.

        How your progress is stored

        Ticking a trophy saves its id in this browser's local storage — nothing leaves your device. Export writes a JSON file you can keep or move to another browser; import reads that file back. The app fetches nothing over the network: it is a static page plus one local data file, and it works with the network off.
